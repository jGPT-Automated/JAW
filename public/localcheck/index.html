01 / Selected contract

The decision, in plain terms

  1. Home / Overview: reuse the expanded drawer design closely: court header, metrics, top check-in/out and Court action, Here Now, Up Next, daily chart and compact Recent Activity. Remove its bottom Games / Players / Feed / Week pills.
  2. Home / Activity: use the selected timeline composition at full available content width, with condensed rows. It includes actual past visits and future planned visits / games. Future and planned activity must remain visibly distinct from actual presence.
  3. Reverse the timeline: at a sample current time of 5 PM, the rows read 8 PM, 7 PM, 6 PM, 5 PM Now, 4 PM, 3 PM…6 AM; the preceding date then continues below, starting from its latest hour. Scroll upward into the future and downward into the past.
  4. Court: the Court button opens the same existing court page. Keep its design and tabs unchanged in this work card. Earlier proposed Court-page removals are not bundled into this direction.
  5. Schedule: remains the place to add future availability and schedule games. Use the selected heatmap composition and a full-width selected-time detail. Home’s timeline does not acquire duplicate planning/create-game controls.

Tab name: Activity, following your explicit selection. “Pulse” remains an optional naming idea, not a new decision to block this direction. The actual product screen should not be labeled Upcoming.
