What the reviewed code already does

The reviewed PR77 source at d018a57 has an outer ScrollView with flex:1/minHeight:0 and content flexGrow:1. It explicitly intends to scroll short phones instead of clipping. Its heatmap is a separate fixed 8-row time window (8 × 28 = 224 points), with later-time controls. The selected-card body uses a fixed 50-point row and a floating action menu remains.

Outer scrolling source · Fixed heatmap window source

In the 375×667 empty-state browser check, the floating plus overlapped the selected-time action. A scroll gesture over that card did not visibly move the page in that state. The internal heatmap had scrollable overflow. This does not establish that all native builds or populated states behave identically.
